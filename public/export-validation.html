Packet counts, media-track/container durations, and a full decoded-frame count come from reading the selected WebM, not from browser presentation callbacks. A decode failure or decoded count different from the saved sequence frame count fails the file gate. Playback drops and callback cadence are device/browser playback metrics only; they do not count missing export frames. Duration deltas use explicit bounds: video half a frame plus half a WebM timebase tick; Opus audio one 20 ms packet plus half a timebase tick and one sample; container uses the stricter applicable track bound. This diagnostic page does not certify audio/video synchronization or inspect every decoded pixel.
